Our Programmes & Projects

"Driving Change Through Innovation, Art, and Education."

Guided by the Zero Waste Hierarchy

All programmes strictly follow the sequence: Refuse → Reduce → Reuse → Recycle → Compost. Burning and landfilling are explicitly rejected in all operations.

Core Programme Areas

Integrated strategic pillars designed to shift systems from linear waste disposal to circular resource recovery.

P1 Flagship Programme

Northern Ghana Plastic Waste Reboot Initiative (NGPWRI)

Plastic waste collection and processing into high-quality flakes and pellets for circular reuse. Provides structured safety training and fair-wage welfare support for over 100 women and youth collectors across Tamale, Kumbungu, and Yendi. Utilises real-time digital collection tracking to monitor diversion volumes.

Target: 1,800 Tonnes Diverted
Supported By: TRANSFORM (Unilever, UK FCDO, EY)
P2 Worker Welfare

E-Waste Safe Handling & Worker Welfare Programme

Formal registration and protection of informal e-waste workers. Supplies personal protective equipment (PPE) and subsidised mechanical stripping tools to eliminate open cable burning. Connects collectors to basic health insurance schemes and financial documentation for economic inclusion.

Focus: Informal Waste Worker Safety & Dignity
P3 Education

Community Zero Waste Education Programme

Grassroots household sensitization and school-based zero waste learning modules. Facilitates market outreach on source separation of materials and trains Zero Waste Community Champions as long-term local advocates.

Focus: Household Separation & Schools Outreach
P4 Community Systems

Zero Waste Communities & Tamale Initiative

Building physical community infrastructure, repair cafes, buy-nothing swap networks, and pilot composting systems. Anchored by the flagship Zero Waste Tamale Initiative, working alongside local assemblies to turn Tamale into Ghana’s first certified Zero Waste City.

Milestone: Zero Waste City Roadmap for Tamale
P5 Policy & Research

Circular Economy Policy & Research Programme

Publishing regional data on e-waste and plastic material flows. Advocating for Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) regulations and amplifying informal worker voices in national legislative discussions.

Focus: Policy Advocacy & Material Flow Data
P6 Capacity Building

Youth & Women Zero Waste Skills Programme

Practical skills training in safe electronics repair, technical collection, zero waste entrepreneurship, and digital marketplace platforms. Features direct apprenticeship linkages into the AppCyclers ecosystem.

Target: 2,000+ Youth & Women Skilled by 2030
