Building Zero Waste Africa

Reclaiming Materials. Restoring Livelihoods. Rebooting Communities.

Our Philosophy View Projects

Our Mission

To build inclusive, community-driven zero waste systems across Africa that protect human health, restore environmental quality, and create dignified livelihoods for those who bear the greatest burden of our throwaway economy.

Our Vision

An Africa where zero waste is not an aspiration but a practice — where no community is poisoned by consumption waste, informal workers are protected and fairly rewarded, and discarded materials source prosperity rather than pollution.

Where Waste Becomes Wealth and Workers Thrive.

Our Core Values

Zero Waste

Designing every programme toward resource conservation without burning or landfilling.

Dignity

Treating every person who touches waste with full human dignity and fair rewards.

Accountability

Putting community impact and environmental protection first in all actions.

Inclusion

Centring women, youth, and marginalised communities at the heart of our systems.

Transparency

Sharing our data, progress, and assessments openly with the public.

Innovation

Deploying modern digital tools, tracking, and evidence-based solutions.
