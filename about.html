About Appcycling Foundation

Advocating for sustainable waste management across Northern Ghana

Our Story

Appcycling Foundation was created to tackle pressing environmental challenges through innovation, grassroots outreach, and structured waste collection systems.

From e-waste management to plastic recovery, we collaborate with local communities and youth to build sustainable practices that protect natural ecosystems and generate green jobs.

Our Mission

To empower local communities with accessible, technology-driven recycling solutions and comprehensive environmental education.

Our Vision

A clean, sustainable Northern Ghana where waste is viewed as a valuable resource for circular economic growth.
